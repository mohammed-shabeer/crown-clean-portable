Crown Clean Laundry — Portable Application
===========================================

A self-contained application. No installation required — just
double-click and use.

HOW TO USE
----------

1. Double-click  CrownCleanLaundry.exe
2. Wait for your browser to open the application automatically
   (address: http://localhost:8000)
3. On first run you will see an "Activate Application" page.
   - Copy the "System ID" shown on that page
   - Send it to your vendor
   - Paste the activation code you receive back
   - The app unlocks permanently on this system
4. Log in with your staff credentials
5. When finished, press "Close Application" in the small window

IMPORTANT
---------

- Keep the launcher window open while using the app.
- The application is licensed to THIS system. Copying it to another
  system will require a new activation from your vendor.

CONTENTS
--------

CrownCleanLaundry.exe   launcher (double-click this)
app\                    the application
runtime\                bundled PHP + MariaDB (required)
data\                   your database (all business data)

Do not delete or separate these folders.

SUPPORT
-------

Contact your vendor for activation codes and support.
